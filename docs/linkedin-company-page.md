## About

CupSpace is the brand of CUPSPACE PTY LTD (ABN 71 690 786 817), an Australian advertising business.

We put a brand on takeaway coffee cups. Local cafés serve those cups. People carry them through the suburbs the advertiser wants to reach, into offices, cars and homes. A QR code on the cup links to the advertiser so an enquiry can be tracked.

CupSpace is for businesses that want local attention, including real estate agents, health practices, trades and other local services, and for cafés that take part in the network.
